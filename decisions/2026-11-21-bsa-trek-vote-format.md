# Decision: Trek vote expands to all 27 treks with flexible month-reply format

**Context:** Karan wanted to run an Instagram/WhatsApp vote letting followers pick which treks they want to do next. The candidate list grew to Bluesheep's full roster of 27 treks (11 Nepal, 16 Indian), spread across very different seasons — Kashmir Great Lakes runs in summer, Pin Parvati from July to November — so a single March/April-style poll couldn't fairly represent the catalog. The counter built for the simpler original poll also had bugs: it misparsed a "Mayday" date entry as the month May, and its leading list was showing rows with zero votes.

**Decision:** Karan decided to include all 27 treks in the vote rather than a seasonal subset, and to switch the reply format to "trek number + month" (e.g. "9 April" for Mardi Himal in April, or "26 August +2" for two people joining), letting respondents pick as many trek/month combinations as they like. If someone picks a month when a trek is snowed in, the nearest good month gets suggested back. The rollout goes out as 4 sequential images — intro, Nepal list, Indian list, closer — on Instagram Stories and WhatsApp Status.

**Consequences:** The vote can now surface real demand across Bluesheep's entire catalog instead of being artificially boxed into one season, but the counting logic had to be rebuilt to parse trek-number-plus-month pairs instead of a single month pick. The 4 images are prepared and already sent to Karan for review — nothing has been sent or posted publicly yet, so the next step is Karan's go-ahead to launch.
