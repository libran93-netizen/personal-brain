# Decision: Defer trek costing to a standardized calculator

**Context:** Adding the Everest Base Camp itinerary tonight raised the question of how to price it, at a point where Karan's itinerary catalog has grown to 19 treks without a consistent way to cost any of them.
**Decision:** No costing goes into individual treks — including EBC — for now. Instead, Karan wants to build a standardized calculator that derives trek cost from all the underlying cost factors at once.
**Consequences:** Itinerary work can keep moving without being blocked on pricing; building the costing calculator becomes a new open item for Blue Sheep Adventures, and will likely need to connect with the commission-and-pricing methodology already in use elsewhere in the business.
